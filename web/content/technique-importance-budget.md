# Technique: an importance budget

[Motion techniques](motion-techniques.md) · Reviewed November 16, 2026

Animating every line as if it were the point is the most common tell of generated motion: nothing arrives because everything does. The Motionmaxxing director guide fixes it with a budget.

```viz motion-techniques/importance-budget
caption: Eight beats rated 1, 2 or 3, drawn as bars. Two of the eight (25 percent) are level 3 and carry the scale, the camera move and the sound.
```

## The rule

Give every beat, and every line inside it, an importance of 1, 2 or 3. At most 25 percent of the beats may be 3. Level 3 gets the scale, the camera move, the sound and the hardest work. Level 1 is small and quick. When two elements fight for attention, delete one before restyling either. On a line with a number, the number is the hero, at least twice the scale of the line.

## How to apply it

1. Add an importance column to the storyboard table ([storyboard first](technique-storyboard-first.md)).
2. Count: with eight beats, at most two may be 3. The hero moment is one of them.
3. Spend the effort in that order: [render and inspect](technique-render-and-inspect.md) the level 3 beats first.
